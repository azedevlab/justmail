# 0. Executive Summary

**Question:** What automation products can be built with n8n for the Azerbaijan market, and which ones can become real SaaS/managed-service businesses?

**Answer:** Azerbaijan is one of the most underserved automation markets adjacent to Europe. The research (August 2026, public sources) found a country where the demand side is enormous and standardized — 1.65M registered taxpayers, 96.8% micro-businesses, running their commerce through Instagram DMs and WhatsApp (97% usage), all compelled through identical state digital rails (e-qaimə e-invoicing, e-taxes, ASAN/SİMA signatures, ISB insurance register, etender procurement) — while the supply side is nearly empty: **no n8n/Make/Zapier agency, no managed-automation provider, no local WhatsApp Business API ecosystem, no vertical conversational-AI specialist was found.** The nearest competitors are Bitrix24/1C integrators (software installers, not outcome sellers), a handful of generic chatbot micro-agencies, and one credible product company (Whelp, omnichannel support).

## The Five Findings That Matter

1. **The #1 automation surface is the Instagram/WhatsApp inbox.** Clinics, salons, travel agencies, training centers, real-estate agents, and shops all confirmed (by direct evidence) to run bookings, sales, and support manually through DMs. An AI receptionist/booking layer in Azerbaijani+Russian is the highest-demand, easiest-to-demo product family — and modern LLMs only recently made Azerbaijani-language automation viable, which is why the space is still empty.

2. **The state built the rails; nobody built on them.** Mandatory e-invoicing routes every B2B invoice through one system; open banking went live October 2025 (13 banks, free Kapital Bank corporate API); SİMA offers embeddable e-signature; etender centralizes procurement. Each is a moat-grade integration that no local vendor has productized.

3. **Pricing works because salaries anchor it.** Admin staff cost 700–1,200 AZN/month in Baku. Automation priced at 150–800 AZN/month against a replaced half-FTE sells on arithmetic, and one agency-model team can stack hundreds of such subscriptions.

4. **Five beachhead verticals share one engine.** Clinics/dental, e-commerce, accounting firms, training centers, and beauty salons — all Market Gaps, all templatable, all reachable through Instagram marketing and referral. ~80% shared workflow DNA (inbox → structured data → calendar/payment/ERP action → lifecycle messaging → owner digest) makes an n8n template business compound.

5. **Structural tailwinds are real:** e-commerce doubling by 2027, Middle Corridor logistics boom (+37% container growth), car market +114% on Chinese-brand entry, tourism +26%, a national AI strategy (2025–2028), startup tax certificates (3-year exemption), and technopark holidays (10-year) — plus a startup ecosystem so under-funded ($2.6M total in 2025) that a revenue-funded automation company faces almost no venture-backed competition.

## The Composite Bet

Execute Phases 1–2 on the five beachheads plus the Tender Watcher SaaS, and a 4–8 person team can realistically reach **80–200k AZN MRR (~$47–118k/month) within 18–24 months**, then expand into open-banking finance automation, logistics/customs, voice AI, and enterprise — with Georgia/Uzbekistan as the regional replication path. The window is open because the enabling tech (Azerbaijani-capable LLMs, open banking, WABA availability) all arrived within the last ~24 months, and no local player has moved yet.
